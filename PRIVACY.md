# Privacy policy: The Macro Block publishing app

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The Macro Block publishes charts and short explainers about Bitcoin and the
economy on Instagram (@themacroblock and @themacroblock.es). This policy
covers the Meta app we use to do that.

## What the app does

The app publishes The Macro Block's own posts (images, Stories and Reels) to
The Macro Block's own Instagram accounts, through Instagram's official API.
It also reads:

- our own accounts' posts and their statistics (likes, comments, reach), to
  see what our readers find useful;
- the public profiles and public posts of a short list of other public
  business accounts, through Instagram's Business Discovery API, to compare
  posting formats and timing. This is information anyone can see on Instagram.

## What the app does not do

- It doesn't log in as anyone, and only acts on The Macro Block's accounts.
- It doesn't like, follow, comment on or message any account.
- It doesn't collect, store, sell or share personal data about anyone who sees
  our posts.
- It doesn't use cookies or tracking, and it has no users other than The
  Macro Block.

## What we keep

We keep a record of our own posts (their Instagram IDs and links) and the
public statistics described above. We keep no personal data about people.

## Our email newsletter

The weekly email edition is sent with Kit (kit.com), not with this app. If you
subscribe, Kit holds your email address under Kit's own privacy policy, and
you can unsubscribe from any email with one click.

## Deleting your data

The app stores no personal data about you, so there's nothing to delete. If
you have any question about your data, or want us to confirm that we hold
none, send a direct message to @themacroblock on Instagram and we'll answer.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
